## Inviting new members to a team 


    1. Navigate to your **Team Settings**
    2. Click **Invite Members**. Input an email address and select a role (Admin or Member).
       * **Members** have access to manage emails, domains and webhooks.
       * **Admins** have all Member permissions plus the ability to invite users, update payments, and delete the team.
    3. The new member will receive an email invitation to join the team.
    
    <Tip>The new invited member will be under **pending invitations**</Tip>

## Swithing between teams 

    After accepting an invite from the account owner, users can switch between teams:

    1. Click on the **team name** in the top left corner of any Strettch cloud page.
    2. A dropdown menu will appear, listing all the teams you belong to.
    3. Select a team to switch between them.

## Change the team member roles

    As an admin of your team, you can change the role of members in your team.

    1. Navigate to your **Team Settings**.
    2. Find the user you want to change.
    3. Select the more options button **...** and choose **edit**.
    <Frame>
     <img src="/images/teams/edit-member-role.png" style={{ borderRadius: '0.2rem' }} />
    </Frame>
    Upon confirmation, your team member will be given the new role.

## Team limits

Two limits apply to every user account:

| Limit | Value | What it counts |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| Teams you own | **2** | Teams where you are the owner, including the default team created with your account |
| Total team memberships | **20** | Every team you are an active member of — teams you own plus teams you joined |

If you try to create a team while already owning 2, creation fails. Accepting an invitation
that would put you over 20 total memberships fails the same way.

Deleting a team frees a slot against both limits.

<Tip>
The owned-teams limit can be raised on your account. Request an increase from your team
settings, or contact our [support team](mailto:cloud@strettch.com) with a short note on
why you need more. The 20-membership ceiling is fixed and is not raised per account.
</Tip>

<Note>
Pending invitations you have not accepted do not count toward either limit. Only active
memberships do.
</Note>

## Leave your Strettch cloud team

   If your team has another admin, you can leave your team by following these steps:

    1. Navigate to your **Team Settings**.
    2. Under members **...** click on next to your name for more options.
    3. Select the **Leave Team** button.
     
    Upon confirmation, you will leave your team.
